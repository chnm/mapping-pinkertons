This comparative dashboard visualizes patterns across all four Pinkerton investigations, allowing side-by-side analysis of activity types, daily timelines, location usage, operative workload, and subject surveillance. Use the toggle to switch between stacked views (which highlight relative proportions) and small multiples (which make it easier to compare individual investigations). Each chart type reveals different facets of how investigations were structured and carried out.
